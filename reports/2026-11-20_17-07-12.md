## Work completed

Added private refresh-based text chat and receipt upload to the existing customer lookup. Added Admin conversation, protected image viewing, replies, approval and rejection, and final manual stock acceptance. Receipt uploads start Pending Verification. Admin explicitly acknowledges actual external funds and records the amount and transfer reference, or enters a rejection reason. Actual payment is external.

PostgreSQL stores screenshot bytes and hashes outside public folders. Upload validation checks PNG/JPEG structure, a 5 MiB limit and an 8,192-pixel bound per side. Images are served only through order-scoped authenticated endpoints as no-store, no-sniff attachments. Chat and receipt API responses avoid internal access digests and transfer references.

Verified money uses decimal strings and integer cents. Order snapshot totals round half up to cents. The deposit is 50% rounded up to the next cent where necessary. Approvals may not exceed the remaining balance. A row lock serializes verification and acceptance. Unique screenshot hashes, unique approved external transfer references and terminal receipt decisions prevent duplicate counting. The order stores acceptance time. The full verified payment indicator remains separate from order acceptance and the workflow has no release action.
